## LinkedIn
We are proud to present **Abstract Algebra Lab**, an interactive undergraduate research project investigating the algebraic foundations of classical cryptographic ciphers.

Rather than treating encryption as isolated algorithmic procedures, our project formalizes each cipher through group-theoretic structures:
• The Caesar Cipher as an additive cyclic group $(C_{26})$
• The Affine Cipher through the unit group $\mathbb{Z}_{26}^\times$ and affine transformations
• Permutations and non-abelian symmetries via the symmetric group $S_4$

Conducted by researchers:
P.V.S. Subramanya Prasad, Ananya Anand, Aditya Datta, Varun Raaj, and Pavani Durga C.
